
# AI LinkedIn Content Automation

## 📌 Project Overview

An AI-powered LinkedIn automation workflow built using **n8n, AI Agents, LLMs, Google Sheets, Google Drive, and LinkedIn integration**.

Automates content research, post creation, AI image generation, content management, and LinkedIn publishing.

- **Project Type:** AI-Powered Workflow Automation
- **Project Status:** Developed & Tested

## 🎯 Business Problem

- Manual content research and post creation require significant time.
- Maintaining consistent LinkedIn content can be challenging.
- Managing content, images, and publishing across multiple tools creates repetitive work.

## ⚡ Key Features

- **Scheduled Automation:** Automatically initiates content workflows.
- **AI Content Generation:** Researches topics and creates LinkedIn posts, captions, and hashtags.
- **AI Image Generation:** Generates relevant visuals using AI-powered image prompts.
- **Google Sheets Integration:** Retrieves inputs and manages content records.
- **Google Drive Integration:** Stores and retrieves generated images.
- **Structured AI Outputs:** Organizes AI-generated content into consistent fields.
- **Conditional Processing:** Controls workflow execution through decision logic.
- **Automated LinkedIn Publishing:** Publishes generated content directly to LinkedIn.

## 🔄 Core Workflow Steps

1. **Schedule Trigger:** Initiates the automation.
2. **Data Retrieval:** Fetches input data from Google Sheets.
3. **AI Content Agent:** Researches topics and generates structured LinkedIn content.
4. **Content Storage:** Saves generated content to Google Sheets.
5. **AI Visual Agent:** Creates prompts and generates relevant images.
6. **Google Drive Integration:** Uploads and retrieves generated visuals.
7. **Content Management:** Updates Google Sheets and processes conditions.
8. **LinkedIn Publishing:** Publishes content through the LinkedIn integration.

## 🧩 Workflow Architecture

**Schedule Trigger → Google Sheets → AI Content Agent → AI Visual Agent → Image Generation → Google Drive → Google Sheets Update → Conditional Logic → LinkedIn Publishing**

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **n8n:** Workflow automation and orchestration
- **AI Agents & LLMs:** Content research and generation
- **AI Image Generation:** Visual content creation
- **Google Sheets:** Content management
- **Google Drive:** Image storage and retrieval
- **LinkedIn API:** Automated post publishing
- **JavaScript:** Data processing
- **Structured Output Parser:** Structured AI responses

## 📈 Business Benefits

- Reduces repetitive content research and preparation.
- Streamlines content creation and publishing.
- Centralizes content and image management.
- Supports consistent LinkedIn content production.
